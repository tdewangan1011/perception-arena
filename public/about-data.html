Data and credits

Your results

The site saves your latest completed result per challenge in this browser. Returning in the same browser profile at the same address lets you view those results again. Clearing the site’s browser data removes its saved results from this device.

Recognition and change-detection sessions, including answers and response times, are processed and stored on the server to score and resume a round. The other challenges score your responses in the browser. Audio challenges play prepared files and do not record from your microphone.
